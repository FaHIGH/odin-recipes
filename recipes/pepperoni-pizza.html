Pepperoni Pizza recipe

You can make a classic, delicious homemade pepperoni pizza with a simple dough, rich tomato sauce, and melty cheese.

Ingredients

  • Pizza Dough: 1.5 cups warm water, 2 1/4 tsp active dry yeast, 2 tsp sugar, 3 tbsp olive oil, 1.5 tsp salt, and 3.75 to 4 cups bread flour.
  • Pizza Sauce: 1 cup pizza sauce or crushed tomatoes mixed with garlic, salt, and oregano.
  • Toppings: 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese, 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese, and 1 cup pepperoni slice

Instructions

  1. Make the dough: Mix warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for 5 minutes until foamy. Add remaining water, olive oil, salt, and flour. Knead for 7 minutes until smooth.
  2. Let it rise:Cover the dough in a greased bowl and let it rise for 1 to 1.5 hours until doubled in size.
  3. Preheat the oven:: Heat your oven to 450°F (230°C), ideally with a pizza stone inside
  4. Assemble the pizza:Punch down the dough and stretch it into a round circle on parchment paper. Spread the pizza sauce, top with mozzarella and parmesan cheeses, and add the pepperoni slices
  5. Bake:Bake: Transfer the pizza on the parchment paper to the hot oven or baking stone. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese bubbles.
